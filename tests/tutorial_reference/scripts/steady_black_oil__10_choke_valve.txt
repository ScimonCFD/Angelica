  • While critical, the rate is fixed by the bean: separator changes do
    not reach the well.  Once P2/P1 > y_c the flow becomes subcritical.
  • The incompressible orifice equation has no sonic limit and uses an
    average-pressure density: here it overestimates the rate by ~8 % at
    48/64" and by ~40 % for the smallest, deeply critical beans.
