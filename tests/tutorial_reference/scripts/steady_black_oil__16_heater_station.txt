Tutorial 16 — A heater station set by its outlet temperature (black-oil)
==============================================================================

Part A — the line with and without the station (ambient 10 °C)
                      Flow  T before  T after  T outlet     Duty
                    (kg/s)      (°C)     (°C)      (°C)     (MW)
  no heating         50.45     60.90    60.92     47.35    0.000
  station 80 °C      51.62     61.32    80.00     61.48    2.668
  Energy balance with the station: 2667.6 kW in, residual +0.034 kW (+2.9e-04 %)

Part C — a station limited to 2.5 MW
   Ambient                  State    Duty  T after  T outlet
      (°C)                           (MW)     (°C)      (°C)
        25  at outlet temperature   2.071    80.00     65.73
        20  at outlet temperature   2.270    80.00     64.32
        15  at outlet temperature   2.469    80.00     62.90
        10           duty limited   2.500    78.84     60.62
         5           duty limited   2.500    77.45     58.16

  • The station turns the second half into a copy of the first.  The oil in
    it is about 16 K warmer (mean 70 against 54 °C) and its viscosity about
    3.5 against 6 mPa·s, so the same pressure drop moves 2.3 % more of it.
  • The colder the ground, the more the first half loses and the more the
    station must give back: about 40 kW per kelvin of ambient here.
  • A station at its limit no longer holds the set point; the solution says
    so ("duty limited") instead of silently delivering colder oil.
