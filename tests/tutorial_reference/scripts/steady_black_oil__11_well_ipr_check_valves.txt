  • Without check valves the manifold pressure exceeds Well 3's reservoir
    pressure and oil flows back into the reservoir.  The check valve shuts
    the well in instead; its wellhead then sits at the reservoir pressure.
  • The linear IPR ignores the gas liberated near the wellbore and
    over-predicts production: 1143 vs 1054 m³/d of oil (+8 %).
