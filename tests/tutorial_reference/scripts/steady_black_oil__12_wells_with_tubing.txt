  • Well 3's static oil-and-water column (25 °API, WOR 0.8, 2 000 m) weighs
    more than its reservoir pressure: it cannot flow without artificial lift.
  • Larger tubing reduces friction in the high-rate wells; the Well model
    solves each well's tubing on its own, so the network converges in a
    few outer iterations even with 2 km two-phase risers.
  • The reservoir fluid warms slightly at the sandface (liquid expanding at
    constant enthalpy) and then cools along the tubing; the slower well
    arrives colder.  Wellhead temperature matters downstream: viscosity,
    wax and hydrate margins all start from it.
  • Hagedorn-Brown suits oil wells, Gray gas and gas-condensate wells; the
    spread between correlations is part of the uncertainty of any well model.
