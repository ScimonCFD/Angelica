  • At these low mixture velocities the gas slips past the liquid everywhere:
    H_L is five to seven times the no-slip fraction C_L.
  • Uphill sections (A, C) hold the most liquid; downhill (B, D) the liquid
    drains faster and H_L is lower, so the descent recovers less elevation
    pressure than the climb cost.
  • The extra liquid also raises the two-phase friction factor: friction,
    not elevation, takes most of the 25 bar.
