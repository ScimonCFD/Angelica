  • Per kg of gas the head curve gives w = ΔP/ρ = 92.4 kJ/kg, the
    reversible compressor ∫v·dP = 108.5 kJ/kg (+17.5 %): ρ at the
    mean pressure but the inlet temperature misses the heating along the
    compression, and the gas leaves 6.5 K hotter than the curve says.
  • A real machine (η = 0.75) needs 33 % more work per kg; the same number
    as a polytropic efficiency means a less efficient machine, hotter still.
