  Reservoir 3.117 kg/s + lift gas 0.510 kg/s = flowline 3.628 kg/s
  Energy balance residual +0.002 kW

  • Without lift the well is dead; a little gas brings it to life.
  • Past the optimum the extra gas adds more friction than it removes
    weight: the oil rate falls although more gas is injected.
  • A deeper valve lightens a longer part of the column, but needs a
    higher injection pressure at the valve (Part B).
