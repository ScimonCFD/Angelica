  • The standard gas and condensate rates come from a flash of each stream to
    standard conditions (Peneloux-corrected liquid volume).
  • Gas cools as it expands into the well (Joule-Thomson) and keeps cooling
    up the tubing; liquid condenses on the way.
  • The condensate well's liquid-laden column is the heavier one: it dies
    against the manifold first; result.well_states says why.
