Part D — heater set by its outlet temperature (45 °C, at most 600 kW)
  Supply (bar)                  State  Duty (kW)  T heater out  T reg. out
            40  at outlet temperature      556.5         45.00       40.42
            50  at outlet temperature      573.9         45.00       34.35
            60  at outlet temperature      594.6         45.00       28.82
            70           duty limited      600.0         43.98       22.41

  • While the regulator controls, the consumers see 20 bar.  The flow still
    rises a little with the supply pressure: a larger let-down cools the gas
    more, and the colder, denser gas passes the distribution line faster.
  • Below about 36 bar the supply line limits the flow: the regulator is
    wide open and the consumers get less than 20 bar.
  • Set by its outlet temperature, the heater's duty follows the supply
    pressure.  At 70 bar the 45 °C set point needs more than 600 kW: the
    heater works at 600 kW and the gas leaves it at 43.98 °C — the same as
    the 600 kW fixed duty of Part B.
