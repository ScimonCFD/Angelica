==============================================================================
Tutorial 15 — A back-pressure regulator holds the separator pressure (black-oil)
==============================================================================

Part A — export 1.5 MPa, with and without the regulator (3.0 MPa)
                     P_sep     Oil       Gas              Regulator
                     (MPa)  (m³/d)  (kSm³/d)
  no regulator       1.537   705.5      83.4                      —
  regulator          3.000   622.3      72.6 controlling, ΔP 1.47 MPa

Part B — export pressure (set point 3.0 MPa)
   Export   P_sep        State     Oil       Gas
    (MPa)   (MPa)               (m³/d)  (kSm³/d)
      0.5   3.000  controlling   622.3      72.6
      1.5   3.000  controlling   622.3      72.6
      2.5   3.000  controlling   622.3      72.6
      3.0   3.012         open   621.4      72.5
      3.5   3.509         open   565.6      68.0
      5.0   5.004         open   459.5      55.4

Part C — set point (export 1.0 MPa)
    P_set   P_sep     Oil       Gas       Well 2
    (MPa)   (MPa)  (m³/d)  (kSm³/d)
      1.5   1.500   707.2      83.7    producing
      2.0   2.000   682.5      80.3    producing
      3.0   3.000   622.3      72.6    producing
      4.0   4.000   533.1      63.9    producing
      5.0   5.000   459.9      55.4    producing

  • The regulator decouples the separator from the export pressure: the
    wells see the same back-pressure whatever happens downstream of it.
  • A higher separator pressure costs oil from these wells; in black-oil it
    gains nothing at the stock tank (see tutorial 14).
