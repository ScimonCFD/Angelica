Part C — a 2-inch oil line
  Separator 1.537 MPa; oil LCV outlet 10.875 MPa: LCV drop -9.338 MPa
  → outlet pressure above the separator at oil_LCV: the liquid needs a pump

  • The separator's pressure is set by what is downstream of its gas;
    its liquid outlets only take what the flash leaves as liquid.
  • In black-oil the stock-tank oil is the wells' oil whatever the
    separator pressure: the model has no stage-separation effect
    (see compositional tutorial 10).
